1. The environment and startup

This space environment lets you explore as Rek’Sai, use a ship, follow a racing circuit and observe animated objects. It contains a base and three planetary zones: crystal-covered rock, forest and an ocean with a wave. Third-person cameras follow the character or ship; free camera and orbit provide other viewpoints.

Requirements: Windows x64 and a graphics driver supporting OpenGL 4.3. Open PROYECTO_GITHUB and run Jugar.cmd or the root LabCGeIHC.exe. Keep Models, Textures, shaders and DLLs beside the executable. Blender and Visual Studio are not required to play. The application finds assets from its own location even when launched from another directory.

After loading, Rek’Sai appears on the base; a separate console may also appear. The clock is in the upper-left corner; the lower HUD identifies mode, zone and state. F9 releases the mouse. A console or running process alone does not establish that the game is visible: a scene like the following must appear.

Figure 1. Release startup: base, avatar and HUD.

2. Implemented controls

Mode, zone and view

F1-F4 do not mean first, second, third or fourth person. F5-F8 change location. C enables a free camera and the wheel changes distance in F4; these view changes do not create a new game experience.

3. Suggested walkthrough

1) Inspect the base and HUD. 2) Press F6 for rock and F1 to walk as Rek’Sai; try WASD and mouse. 3) Use 2 for the capsule door, 6 to play the staged rocket and 7 to reset it. 4) Press F5 for forest. 5) Press F2 for racing; W/S control speed and A/D change lane. 6) In F3, fly between planets. Approaching an atmosphere changes zone; E is contextual and portals provide exits. In the ocean, the ship’s route follows the wave toward its exit.

In F4, Tab cycles through available D1, hangar, rock, forest, satellite and base-rocket targets. At the hangar, 4 starts the Fighter departure/return and 5 demonstrates the door. At the satellite, 3 folds the panels. F1 returns to on-foot travel on the last planet. F8 returns to space.

Figure 2. Hangar viewed in F4.

Troubleshooting

If no window appears, check the complete DLL and asset set and inspect the --probar-release log; a dependency failure can terminate before any text appears. Use F9 if the pointer is captured. If lost, use F8 or F6/F1. If a key is pressed during loading, wait and press it again. The first visit may load models. Hardware without OpenGL 4.3 is not supported.

What I worked on

My main contribution was Rek’Sai: preparing the parts and pivots, adjusting the gait and connecting movement to the controls. I also worked on keyboard and mouse input and the HUD, which displays the mode, zone and player state. During integration, I checked that my contribution worked with the team’s cameras and environments.

This contribution can be followed in src/game/avatar/Avatar.cpp, Input.cpp and src/ui/Hud.cpp, together with their integration in Main.cpp. The history also records the later integration of TARS in the ocean. Engine and world modules retain the work of A and B. Submission preparation changes and automated tests were completed with Codex assistance, as recorded in the work log.
